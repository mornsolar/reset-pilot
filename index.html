An honest experiment, not a product

A one-week phone reset. I'm running it myself — and I'd like a few more people to run it with me.

Not an app. Not a subscription. A structured week of declaring your phone use before you pick it up, then locking it away for an equal stretch after — to see whether the change actually sticks once the structure is gone.

What this actually is

Before each time you pick up your phone, you declare three things — out loud or written down: why you need it right now, which app(s) you'll use, and how long you need. You use it freely for that window. When the window closes, the phone gets locked away for an equal length of time before you can pick it up again — no renegotiating, no "just five more minutes."

You choose the lock mechanism yourself — a timer, a screen-time limit, a lockbox, handing it to someone else for a stretch. The method doesn't matter. The ritual does: declare before, lock after, no exceptions.

"More like training one's muscle, not an app that should be on forever — it will cause plenty of inconvenience for the locked-up period, and that's only acceptable because it's bounded and you chose it."

Why I'm asking you to join, not buy

I tested an early, informal version of this on myself first — my daughter was the one who, mid-experiment, taught me something I hadn't planned for. You can read that story here →.

What I haven't done yet is run the full, structured one-week version properly — and I don't know if what changed for me in that small moment holds up over a real week, for people who aren't me. That's genuinely the open question. This page exists to find a few people willing to find out with me, not to sell something I've already proven works.

What I'm asking of you

  1. 1

    Commit to one week of the declare-then-lock ritual, plus a short baseline day before it starts.

  2. 2

    Notice and jot down what happens — a few lines a day is enough.

  3. 3

    Talk to me honestly afterward about what changed, what didn't, and whether it stuck once the week ended.

This is free, and it's genuinely small — a handful of people, because I want to pay attention to each person's week, not because of manufactured scarcity. I'll reach out directly to coordinate timing once a small group is together.
